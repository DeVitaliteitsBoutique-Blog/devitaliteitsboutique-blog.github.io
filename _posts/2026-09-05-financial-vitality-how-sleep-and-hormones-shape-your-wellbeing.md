- Difficulty focusing, which impacts both work and financial planning

## Stress and Hormones: The Mind-Body Connection for Women

Stress is not just a mental state—it’s a physical response. Lack of sleep, processed foods, and constant pressure can flood your body with cortisol, the “fight-or-flight” hormone. Over time, this can disrupt hormone balance, weaken your immune system, and even affect your energy to manage daily finances.

But here’s the good news: with the right mindset, stress can be a positive force. Seeing challenges as opportunities for growth helps your body produce beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone—both vital for energy, mood, and resilience.

### Practical Tips to Support Your Hormone Balance and Sleep Health

As a sleeptherapist and hormone expert at De Vitaliteitsboutique, I recommend these simple steps:

- **Prioritize Sleep:** Create a calming evening routine and stick to regular sleep times.  
- **Breathe Mindfully:** Gentle breathing exercises can calm your stress response, supporting hormonal balance.  
- **Choose Nourishing Foods:** Opt for whole, unprocessed meals that stabilize blood sugar and hormones.  
- **Move Gently:** Regular, enjoyable movement supports both mood and hormone health.  
- **Strengthen Your Immune System:** Adequate rest, balanced nutrition, and stress management keep your body strong.
