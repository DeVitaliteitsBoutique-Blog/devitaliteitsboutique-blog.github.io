## The Link Between Vitality, Sleep, and Hormones

Hormone problems don’t appear overnight. They can be influenced by chronic stress, poor sleep, processed foods, or a lack of movement. As a **sleepcoach** and **hormone expert** at De Vitaliteitsboutique, I see how these factors intertwine. Sleep, in particular, is crucial for hormone balance. When we sleep deeply and restoratively, our bodies produce growth hormone and regulate cortisol—key players in energy, mood, and menstrual regularity.

Insomnia or sleeping problems disrupt this cycle, leaving you tired, irritable, and more sensitive to stress. Over time, this can lead to burnout, especially for female entrepreneurs or busy women balancing work and family.

## Stress Is More Than Just a Feeling

Stress is not just a mental state—it’s a physical response that impacts your hormones. Short-term stress can be motivating, but chronic stress (from poor sleep, irregular meals, or constant rushing) increases cortisol and depletes beneficial hormones. This creates a cycle where your body feels constantly “on alert,” making it harder to relax, sleep well, or maintain a healthy cycle.

The good news? By shifting your mindset, stress can become a source of strength. At De Vitaliteitsboutique, we encourage viewing challenges as opportunities for growth. This positive approach can boost DHEA and growth hormone, supporting resilience and a sense of well-being.

### How can stress therapy help with menopause and PMS symptoms?

Stress therapy addresses chronic stress that disrupts hormone levels by lowering cortisol and supporting beneficial hormones such as DHEA. This can alleviate symptoms of menopause and PMS by promoting hormonal harmony and resilience.
