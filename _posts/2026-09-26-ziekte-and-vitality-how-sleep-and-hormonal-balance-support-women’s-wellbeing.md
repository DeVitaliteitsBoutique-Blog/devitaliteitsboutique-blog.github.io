## The Hidden Connection Between Ziekte, Sleep, and Hormones in The Netherlands

Women’s bodies are beautifully complex, and hormones play a starring role in energy, mood, and overall wellbeing. When hormone balance is disrupted—by stress, poor sleep, menopause, or PMS—symptoms like insomnia, low energy, and emotional ups and downs can arise. Over time, these imbalances can leave you feeling unwell, drained, and more susceptible to ziekte.

**Vitality** is the foundation that helps women navigate these challenges. Quality sleep is essential for hormone regulation, supporting the production of beneficial hormones such as growth hormone and DHEA, which aid in resilience and recovery.

## Stress as a Physical and Mental Response Affecting Women’s Health

Stress isn’t just a mental state—it’s a physical reaction that can impact every part of your body. Poor sleep, highly processed foods, and constant pressure can amplify stress, affecting your immune system and your hormone balance. Over time, chronic stress can trigger or worsen ziekte, disrupting sleep and further throwing hormones off balance.

But there’s good news: a positive stress mindset can transform the way your body responds. By viewing challenges as opportunities for growth, you can actually boost the production of helpful hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. This shift not only helps with burnout prevention for women, but also supports a healthier work-life balance.
