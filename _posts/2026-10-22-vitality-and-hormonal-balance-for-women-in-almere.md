## Stress as a Physical and Mental Trigger for Hormonal Problems

Stress isn’t just a feeling—it’s a physical and mental response that directly impacts hormonal health. Factors like irregular sleep, processed foods, and lack of movement can heighten stress and disrupt hormone balance. But stress doesn’t always have to be the villain. By shifting to a positive stress mindset, you can begin to view challenges as opportunities for personal growth. This approach can actually boost beneficial hormones, helping you feel more energetic and focused.

### Common Triggers for Hormonal Imbalance in Women in Almere:

- **Poor Sleep:** Leads to increased cortisol and throws off the balance of estrogen and progesterone.
- **Diet:** Highly processed foods can spike insulin and disrupt hormonal harmony.
- **Chronic Stress:** Keeps the body in a heightened state, draining energy reserves and impacting menstrual cycles.
- **Lack of Movement:** Reduces the production of mood-boosting and stress-relieving hormones.
