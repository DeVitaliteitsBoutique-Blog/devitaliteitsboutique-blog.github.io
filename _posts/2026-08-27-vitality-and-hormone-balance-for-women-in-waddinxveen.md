## Stress as a Physical and Mental Response

Stress is not just a feeling—it’s a complex reaction within your body and mind. Whether triggered by a busy workday, processed foods, or restless nights, stress can disrupt hormone balance. Chronic stress increases cortisol levels, which may lead to hormonal problems, insomnia, and even impact your immune system strength.

But stress isn’t always the enemy. With the right mindset and support, stress can actually boost beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone, which support vitality and help your body adapt and grow stronger. Viewing challenges as opportunities for growth is a key part of managing stress in a healthy, positive way.
