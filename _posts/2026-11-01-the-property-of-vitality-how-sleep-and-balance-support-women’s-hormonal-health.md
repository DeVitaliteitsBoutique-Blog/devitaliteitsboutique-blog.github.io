## How Stress Influences Hormones

Stress isn’t just a mental challenge—it’s a whole-body response. As a stress therapist and holistic hormone coach, we observe how stress can magnify hormonal symptoms. Poor sleep, processed foods, and a lack of self-care can push stress hormones like cortisol out of balance. However, not all stress is harmful. A positive stress mindset—seeing challenges as opportunities—can actually boost beneficial hormones such as DHEA and growth hormone. This perspective helps you build resilience and transform stress into a catalyst for personal growth.
