## Understanding How Vitality and Sleep Impact Hormones

Hormones play a powerful role in regulating energy, mood, and even how well you sleep. When hormones such as estrogen and progesterone fluctuate, as they do during PMS or menopause, women can experience insomnia, low energy, and emotional ups and downs. Poor sleep and constant stress can make these hormone problems worse, creating a cycle that feels impossible to break.

That’s why supporting your body’s natural rhythms is so important. A sleepcoach or sleeptherapist doesn’t just address insomnia—they look at the bigger picture, helping you restore both deep rest and daytime vitality. By balancing sleep and daily energy, you give your hormones a chance to reset, reducing symptoms and helping you feel more vibrant.

## The Stress Response and Your Hormones

Stress is both a physical and mental reaction to life’s demands. While some stress is unavoidable, chronic stress can disrupt hormone balance, especially when paired with poor sleep or processed food. This can lead to burnout, more severe PMS, or even irregular cycles.

However, not all stress is harmful. Building a positive stress mindset—seeing challenges as opportunities for growth—can actually boost beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. These support resilience, repair, and even anti-aging. At De Vitaliteitsboutique, our stress coach and holistic hormone coach help you reframe stress, harnessing it for personal growth and wellbeing.
