## How Stress Impacts Hormones and Energy for Women

Stress isn’t just a feeling—it’s a physical and mental response that directly affects sleep and hormones. Poor sleep, irregular meals, or processed foods can all add to stress, making symptoms like fatigue and mood swings worse. But did you know that your mindset towards stress matters just as much?

Adopting a positive stress mindset—seeing challenges as opportunities—can actually boost beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. These hormones support your immune system, repair tissues, and help you recover from stress. A stress coach or breathing therapist can help you harness this power, turning stress into a tool for growth.
