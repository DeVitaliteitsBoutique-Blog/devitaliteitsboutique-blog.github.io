## Sleep and Hormones: The Restorative Connection for Dutch Women

Sleep is a natural reset button for your hormones. Quality sleep supports the production of essential hormones like estrogen, progesterone, and melatonin, which regulate your menstrual cycle, mood, and stress response. Lack of sleep, on the other hand, can trigger a cascade of issues—think increased cortisol (the stress hormone), more intense PMS, and disrupted cycles.

A dedicated sleepcoach or sleeptherapist based in the Netherlands can guide you in:

- Creating a relaxing bedtime routine
- Managing screen time and blue light exposure
- Using breathing techniques to fall asleep faster
- Identifying nutrition tweaks that promote deeper sleep

These small steps can make a big difference for women struggling with insomnia or hormonal imbalances.
