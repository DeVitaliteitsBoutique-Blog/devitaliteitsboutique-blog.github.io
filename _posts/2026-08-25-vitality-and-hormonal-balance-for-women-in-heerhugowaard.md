## Stress: More Than Just a Feeling

Stress is a physical and mental response that affects every system in the body. Factors like insufficient sleep, processed foods, or constant pressure can tip the balance of hormones such as cortisol, which in turn can worsen **insomnia**, fatigue, and emotional ups and downs. As a **stress therapist** and **stress coach**, our approach recognizes that while stress is natural, it doesn’t have to be harmful.

### Embracing a Positive Stress Mindset

Did you know that how you perceive stress can influence your body’s response? Viewing challenges as opportunities for growth can actually boost beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. This positive stress mindset supports resilience and helps your body adapt, rather than break down.
