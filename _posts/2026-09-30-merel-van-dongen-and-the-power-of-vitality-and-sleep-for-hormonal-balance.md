## Why Vitality and Sleep Are Key for Women’s Hormonal Health in The Netherlands

Our bodies are beautifully complex, with hormones orchestrating everything from our menstrual cycles to our mood. When hormones like estrogen and progesterone fluctuate, symptoms can appear—especially during PMS, menopause, or stressful periods. This is where a holistic approach matters.

- **Vitality** is about more than just feeling awake; it’s about having the sustained energy, mental clarity, and emotional balance to enjoy life’s moments.
- **Quality sleep** is a cornerstone of hormonal health. As a sleepcoach and sleeptherapist based in The Netherlands, we see that sleep problems and insomnia often make hormone problems worse, leading to persistent tiredness, irritability, and even difficulty concentrating.

## The Hidden Role of Stress in Hormonal Imbalances for Women Entrepreneurs

Stress is not only a mental state—it’s a physical response that disrupts hormonal balance. When stress lingers, it can trigger the release of cortisol, which in turn impacts sleep, immunity, and even appetite.

Factors like:
- Poor sleep
- Consuming processed foods
- Skipping movement or exercise

all contribute to stress levels. Over time, this can intensify PMS, menopause symptoms, or general feelings of burnout. This is especially relevant for women entrepreneurs and those juggling work-life balance in The Netherlands.

But there’s good news: not all stress is bad. By cultivating a positive stress mindset—seeing challenges as opportunities for growth—you can actually boost beneficial hormones such as DHEA and growth hormone, which support resilience and repair.
