### What does a sleepcoach do for women with hormonal imbalances?  
A sleepcoach specializes in creating personalized sleep routines that improve sleep quality, which is essential for hormone regulation. Better sleep supports balance in hormones such as cortisol, melatonin, and estrogen, helping to reduce symptoms like fatigue and mood swings.

### How can stress therapy help with menopause symptoms?  
Stress therapy teaches practical tools to manage and reduce chronic stress, which often worsens menopause symptoms like hot flashes, irritability, and sleep disturbances. Managing stress supports hormonal balance and helps maintain vitality during this transition.

### What is holistic hormone coaching, and how does it differ from traditional hormone therapy?  
Holistic hormone coaching focuses on natural and lifestyle-based approaches to balance hormones, including nutrition, movement, sleep, and stress management. Unlike traditional hormone therapy that uses medications or supplements, holistic coaching empowers women to take control through sustainable lifestyle changes.

### Can breathing therapy improve sleep and reduce insomnia?  
Yes, breathing therapy helps calm the nervous system and reduce stress, making it easier to fall asleep and stay asleep. It supports relaxation, improves oxygen flow, and can be a powerful tool against insomnia.
