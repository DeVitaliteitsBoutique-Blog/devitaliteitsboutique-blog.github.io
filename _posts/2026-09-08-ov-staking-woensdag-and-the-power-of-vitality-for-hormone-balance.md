- **Fatigue and low energy**
- **Mood swings**
- **Difficulty concentrating**
- **Irregular menstrual cycles**
- **Worsened PMS or menopause symptoms**

It’s not just about feeling tired—sleeping problems can make hormone issues worse. ## Stress: More Than Just a Mental Challenge

Stress isn’t only a feeling—it’s a physical reaction. Poor sleep, processed foods, and lack of movement can all increase stress on the body, influencing hormones and leaving you feeling drained.

But here’s a powerful mindset shift: **not all stress is bad**. When you view challenges like ov staking woensdag as opportunities for growth, your body produces beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. This helps you adapt, become more resilient, and even supports hormone balance.
