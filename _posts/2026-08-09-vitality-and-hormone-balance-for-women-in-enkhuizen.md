## The Sleep and Hormone Connection

Sleep is so much more than rest. As a sleepcoach and sleeptherapist, we often meet women who struggle to fall asleep or wake up feeling unrested. Insomnia or disrupted sleep can quickly throw your hormones out of balance, making existing issues like PMS or menopause symptoms even harder to manage.

Quality sleep supports healthy hormone production, including melatonin, estrogen, and progesterone. When sleep suffers, women are more likely to experience:

- Increased fatigue  
- Irritability or mood swings  
- Difficulty concentrating  
- Irregular menstrual cycles  

Our sleepcoaching approach in Enkhuizen focuses on practical changes—like adjusting bedtime routines and nutrition—to restore restful nights and balanced hormones.

## Stress: More Than Just a Feeling

Stress isn’t only an emotional response. It’s a physical reaction that starts in your body and is heavily influenced by factors like poor sleep, lack of movement, or eating processed foods. As a stress coach and stress therapist, we see how easily these factors can trigger hormonal imbalances, worsening symptoms such as hot flashes, low energy, or cravings.

But stress doesn’t have to be the enemy. By adopting a positive mindset towards stress—seeing challenges as opportunities to grow—your body can actually boost beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone. This mindset shift helps build resilience and supports your overall vitality.
