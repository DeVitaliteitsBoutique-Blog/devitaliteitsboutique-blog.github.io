## Understanding Belasting Stress and Hormonal Imbalance in The Netherlands

Belasting isn’t just mental overload. It’s also a physical response, affecting your whole body—especially your hormones. When stress lingers, your body produces more cortisol, which can throw off the delicate balance of female hormones. This can lead to sleeping problems, PMS, menopausal symptoms, and even worsen hormone-related issues like irregular cycles or insomnia.

The way we manage belasting is shaped by daily habits common in Dutch women:
- **Poor sleep** disrupts the body’s natural hormone cycles, making you feel tired and irritable.
- **Processed foods** and skipped meals can cause blood sugar crashes, amplifying stress and mood swings.
- **Lack of movement** and shallow breathing keep your body in a constant state of alert, making it harder to unwind.
