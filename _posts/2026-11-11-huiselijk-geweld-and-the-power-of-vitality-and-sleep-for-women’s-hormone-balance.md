## How Sleep and Vitality Influence Your Hormones

Your body’s hormones are sensitive to daily rhythms and stressors. Poor sleep and low vitality can trigger hormone imbalances, making symptoms like insomnia, irritability, and exhaustion even worse. When you’re not sleeping well, your body produces more stress hormones such as cortisol, while beneficial hormones like DHEA and growth hormone decrease. This can disrupt your menstrual cycle, worsen PMS, and make menopause symptoms more intense.

**Restorative sleep** is not just about hours in bed—it’s about quality and the right sleep-wake pattern. As a sleepcoach and sleeptherapist, De Vitaliteitsboutique guides you to identify habits and beliefs that hinder good rest, helping you build routines that restore your energy and hormone balance.

## Stress as a Physical and Mental Response

Stress is more than a feeling—it’s a whole-body reaction that affects hormones, immune system, and even digestion. Chronic stress, especially from traumatic experiences or ongoing challenges, can make it harder for your body to regulate itself. Factors like poor sleep, processed foods, and lack of movement can amplify these effects.

But did you know that your **mindset towards stress** can change your body’s response? When you view challenges as opportunities for growth, your body produces more DHEA and growth hormone—both vital for resilience and healing. This positive stress mindset is a powerful tool for women seeking hormonal balance and emotional recovery.
